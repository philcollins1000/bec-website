title: Fun on the Green 2026

description: |-
  The sun did its bit for us this year. Saturday 11 July brought a scorching afternoon to the Green, and the village turned out in force — lots of fun and games, lots of laughter, picnics and bubbly, and plenty of music. This year's World Cup theme gave the day its colour, with flags and bunting strung around the marquee, and a live band that soon had children and grown-ups alike up and dancing on the grass.

  The games were as popular as ever — a constant stream of children (and a fair few grown-up children!) queued to try Welly-Wanging, Splat-the-Rat and the football game, which drew a long line of would-be strikers hoping to beat the keeper.

  Our thanks to everyone who turned out on a very hot Friday evening to put up the marquees, and again on Sunday morning to take them down — and to all the kind villagers who ran the games and kept the whole afternoon going.
